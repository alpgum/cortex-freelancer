# Local Payment Methods Research — EG / PK / NG / TR

## Egypt (EG)

### Primary Methods
- **Vodafone Cash**: Most popular mobile wallet. ~30M users. Instant transfers, low fees (1-2%). Supported by Upwork via Payoneer integration.
- **Instapay**: National instant payment network by CBE. Free bank-to-bank transfers. Growing adoption among freelancers.
- **Payoneer**: De facto standard for Egyptian freelancers on Upwork/Fiverr. 2% currency conversion fee. Withdraw to local bank or Vodafone Cash.
- **Wise (TransferWise)**: Competitive FX rates (0.5-1.5% fee). Direct to Egyptian bank accounts. Slower than Vodafone Cash (1-3 days).
- **Bank Transfer (SWIFT)**: Available but expensive ($15-25 incoming wire fee + poor FX spread). Used mainly for large payments.

### Challenges
- EGP volatility makes USD-denominated earnings attractive but conversion timing matters
- Informal economy means many freelancers lack formal banking relationships
- Capital controls can delay large transfers (>$10,000)

### Recommendations
- Default to Payoneer → Vodafone Cash for speed and low fees
- Wise for larger amounts where FX rate matters more than speed
- Advise holding USD when possible given EGP depreciation trends

## Pakistan (PK)

### Primary Methods
- **JazzCash**: Largest mobile wallet (~85M accounts). Instant transfers. Supported for local payments.
- **Easypaisa**: Second largest mobile wallet (~50M accounts). Similar functionality to JazzCash.
- **Payoneer**: Primary international withdrawal method. Direct to local bank (PKR). 2% conversion fee.
- **Wise**: Growing adoption. Competitive rates. Direct to Pakistani bank accounts.
- **Bank Transfer (SWIFT)**: Available via SBP (State Bank of Pakistan). $15-20 incoming fee. 2-5 day settlement.

### Challenges
- Government policy on IT exports provides tax exemption, encouraging freelancing
- SBP regulations require inward remittance certificates for foreign earnings
- PayPal is not available in Pakistan — major friction point
- Dollar account restrictions make Payoneer the primary bridge

### Recommendations
- Payoneer → local bank transfer is the most reliable path
- JazzCash/Easypaisa for smaller, faster local disbursements
- Wise as alternative for better FX rates on larger amounts

## Nigeria (NG)

### Primary Methods
- **Payoneer**: Most used by Nigerian freelancers. Withdraw to Naira account. 2% conversion + potential bank charges.
- **Wise**: Direct to Nigerian bank accounts. Better rates than Payoneer for amounts >$200.
- **Chipper Cash**: Pan-African fintech. USD wallet + NGN conversion. Growing freelancer adoption.
- **Flutterwave**: Payment infrastructure used by businesses. Barter virtual USD cards available.
- **Bank Transfer (SWIFT)**: Available but CBN regulations can cause delays. $10-25 fees.

### Challenges
- CBN (Central Bank of Nigeria) multiple exchange rate windows create uncertainty
- Naira volatility (parallel vs. official rate) significantly affects real earnings
- PayPal has limited functionality (receive-only restrictions)
- Crypto (USDT) widely used informally but regulatory grey area

### Recommendations
- Payoneer remains the safest, most established option
- Wise for cost-conscious freelancers on larger payments
- Chipper Cash for pan-African freelancers working across borders
- Monitor CBN policy changes as they directly impact withdrawal options

## Turkey (TR)

### Primary Methods
- **Wise**: Primary choice for Turkish freelancers. Competitive EUR/USD → TRY rates. Direct to Turkish bank (IBAN).
- **Payoneer**: Supported for platform withdrawals. 2% conversion fee. Well-established.
- **Bank Transfer (SWIFT)**: Direct international wire to Turkish banks. $15-25 fees. IBAN-based system.
- **Papara**: Digital wallet popular among younger generation. TRY-denominated. Good for local transfers.
- **PayPal**: Available in Turkey (re-enabled 2024) but limited. Withdrawal to Turkish bank possible.

### Challenges
- TRY depreciation (>50% in 2 years) makes currency timing critical
- TCMB (Central Bank) FX regulations affect large conversions
- Withholding tax applies on some international service payments
- Banking infrastructure is strong but SWIFT fees are high

### Recommendations
- Wise for best FX rates on regular withdrawals
- Payoneer as reliable backup, especially for platform-sourced income
- Consider holding USD/EUR in multi-currency accounts when possible
- Papara for domestic freelancer-to-client payments
