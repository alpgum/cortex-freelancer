Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about Cortex Pro

Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Cancel your subscription at any time from your account. Your access continues until the end of your billing period. No penalties, no lock-in contracts.
What's the money-back guarantee?
We offer a 7-day no-questions-asked refund policy. If Cortex isn't working for you within the first 7 days, email us and we'll refund you in full.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex) through Stripe. Payments are processed securely — we never see or store your card details.
Can I switch between monthly and annual?
Yes. You can upgrade from monthly to annual at any time and we'll pro-rate the difference. Switching from annual to monthly takes effect at the end of your annual billing cycle.
Do I need to install anything?
You'll need OpenClaw installed (free, open-source). After purchase, you'll get a setup script that installs and configures all your agents in under 5 minutes.
How do refunds work?
If you're not satisfied within the first 7 days, email us at hello@cortexfreelancer.com and we'll issue a full refund within 3-5 business days. No forms, no hoops. Request a refund →
Do you offer team or agency pricing?
Not yet, but it's on our roadmap. Right now each Pro subscription is for one user. If you need multiple seats, contact us and we'll work out a custom deal.
Are there any discounts available?
Yes! Annual billing saves you 28% compared to monthly. We also offer discounts for students and freelancers in developing countries — reach out to us with proof of eligibility.
Are taxes included in the price?
Prices shown are exclusive of tax. Depending on your location, applicable taxes (such as VAT or sales tax) may be added at checkout. Tax is automatically calculated by Stripe where required by law.
Is there a contract or commitment?
No contracts, ever. Cortex Pro is month-to-month (or annual if you choose). You can cancel anytime from your dashboard with one click. There are no cancellation fees, no lock-in periods, and no penalties. You stay in control.
Can I try Pro before paying?
Yes! We offer a 7-day free trial with full access to all Pro features. No credit card required to start. If you love it, upgrade at any time. If not, you'll automatically return to the Free plan with no charge.
Do you offer team or agency plans?
Team plans are coming soon! We're actively building multi-seat support with shared templates, team dashboards, and centralized billing. Join our waitlist above to be notified first. In the meantime, each Pro subscription works great for individual freelancers and solopreneurs.
Is my data safe?
Absolutely. Your data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest. We use Firebase and Stripe for authentication and payments — both are SOC 2 compliant. We never sell your data, and you can export or delete your data at any time from your dashboard. Our AI tools process your input in real-time but don't store or train on your content.
What happens if I cancel?
If you cancel, your Pro access continues until the end of your current billing period — you've already paid for it, so you keep it. After that, you'll return to the Free plan. Your saved data (invoices, proposals, analyses) stays safe and accessible. You can re-subscribe anytime to get Pro back instantly.
What if Cortex doesn't work for me?
We want you to be completely satisfied. Start with a 7-day free trial — no risk at all. If you do subscribe and aren't happy within the first 7 days of paid billing, email us at hello@cortexfreelancer.com and we'll give you a full refund, no questions asked. See our refund policy →
