Our Story

Why we built Cortex

We spent years freelancing on Upwork, Fiverr, and direct clients. We loved the work. We hated the admin — chasing invoices, writing the same proposals over and over, losing money on payment fees we didn't understand, and spending hours on tasks that weren't billable.

So we built the tool we wished existed: an AI-powered business manager that handles proposals, invoicing, contracts, rate optimization, and client communication — so freelancers can focus on doing great work.

Cortex launched in 2026 and is now used by 2,400+ freelancers across 50+ countries. Every feature we ship comes from real freelancer pain points — because we're freelancers too.
