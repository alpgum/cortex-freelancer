Cortex Freelancer vs AND CO (Fiverr Workspace): Honest Comparison

AND CO was acquired by Fiverr and rebranded as Fiverr Workspace. Here is how it compares to Cortex Freelancer for managing your freelance business.

Note: AND CO was acquired by Fiverr in 2018 and rebranded to Fiverr Workspace. Some features have changed since the acquisition. This comparison reflects Fiverr Workspace as it exists today.

Quick Verdict

Fiverr Workspace (formerly AND CO) is a decent basic tool if you are already on Fiverr — it is free for Fiverr sellers and handles invoicing and contracts well enough. But it is a lightweight tool with no AI capabilities and limited functionality outside the Fiverr ecosystem. Cortex Freelancer is a more powerful, AI-driven platform built for freelancers who want to grow their business independently, with global payment support and intelligent automation.

Key Differences

Independent vs Platform-Dependent

The biggest distinction: Fiverr Workspace is designed to keep you in the Fiverr ecosystem. It works best for Fiverr sellers and loses much of its appeal if you find clients elsewhere. Cortex Freelancer is platform-independent — it works for freelancers regardless of where they find their clients, whether that is Upwork, LinkedIn, referrals, or cold outreach.

AI Intelligence vs Basic Tools

Cortex Freelancer provides 8 specialized AI agents that actively help you grow your business — analyzing scopes for red flags, calculating optimal rates, reviewing your portfolio, drafting client communications, and optimizing payments. Fiverr Workspace offers basic operational tools (invoicing, contracts, time tracking) without any AI layer.

Payment Fees

Fiverr takes a 20% commission on work done through their platform, on top of standard payment processing fees. Even using Fiverr Workspace for off-platform work, the fee structure is not optimized. Cortex Freelancer's integration with Cenoa keeps cross-border payment fees under 1% — a massive difference for freelancers sending or receiving international payments.

Time Tracking & Expenses

To be fair, Fiverr Workspace does include built-in time tracking and expense management, which are useful for freelancers billing by the hour. Cortex Freelancer focuses more on the strategic side — optimizing your rates, improving your proposals, and managing client relationships through AI. Both approaches have merit depending on your workflow.

Who Should Choose What?

Choose Cortex Freelancer if you…

  • Find clients outside of Fiverr (or plan to)
  • Want AI agents to help optimize every aspect of your business
  • Work with international clients and need low-fee payments
  • Want portfolio review and rate optimization powered by AI
  • Are serious about growing beyond a single platform

Choose AND CO / Fiverr Workspace if you…

  • Do most of your work through Fiverr and want free tools
  • Need basic invoicing and contracts without paying extra
  • Want built-in time tracking for hourly billing
  • Are happy within the Fiverr ecosystem
  • Prefer simplicity over advanced features
