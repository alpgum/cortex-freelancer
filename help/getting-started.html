Getting Started

Everything you need to know to set up your account and start using Cortex Freelancer in minutes.

Quick Start Guide

1

Create Your Account

Click "Get Started" on the homepage or visit /app. Sign up with Google or email — it's free and takes under 30 seconds. No credit card required.

2

Analyze Your Profile

Paste your Upwork profile URL or fill in the manual form with your skill, rate, country, and experience level. Our AI will generate a detailed analysis with scores and recommendations.

3

Explore Free Tools

The Free Kit gives you access to the fee calculator, rate calculator, contract reviewer, scope analyzer, and ad generator. Try them out to see how Cortex can help your business.

4

Chat with Your AI Advisor

Open the AI Chat to ask questions about your freelance business — pricing strategies, client communication, contract terms, and more.

5

Upgrade to Pro (Optional)

Unlock all 10+ tools, unlimited analyses, proposal writer, invoice generator, email writer, and priority support for $29/month. See pricing.

Pro Tip

Start with a profile analysis — it gives you a personalized roadmap of what to improve. Then use the specific tools (rate calculator, proposal writer) to act on those recommendations.

Understanding Your Dashboard

After signing in, your dashboard is your home base. Here's what you'll find:

  • Profile Analysis — your latest profile score with category breakdowns
  • Quick Actions — one-click access to all tools
  • AI Chat — your freelance business advisor
  • Recent Activity — history of your analyses and generated documents

Free vs Pro

The free tier is generous and includes core tools. Here's what Pro adds:

  • Unlimited profile analyses (free plan: 1)
  • Proposal writer — generate client-ready proposals in seconds
  • Invoice generator — create professional invoices
  • Email writer — craft client emails with AI
  • SOW generator — create detailed statements of work
  • Project brief creator — structured briefs for clients
  • Portfolio reviewer — get feedback on your portfolio
  • Client red flag detector — vet potential clients
  • Priority support — faster response times
