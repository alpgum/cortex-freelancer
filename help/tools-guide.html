Tools Guide

A complete guide to every tool available on Cortex Freelancer. Learn what each tool does and how to get the most out of it.

Analysis Tools

Profile Analyzer Free (1x) Unlimited with Pro

Paste your Upwork profile URL or enter your details manually. The AI analyzes your profile across multiple dimensions — headline, summary, skills, rate positioning, portfolio, and more — and gives you a score out of 100 with specific improvement recommendations.

How to use: Go to the dashboard, paste your URL or fill the manual form, and click "Analyze". Results appear in under 30 seconds.

Open Profile Analyzer

Portfolio Reviewer Pro

Get AI-powered feedback on your freelance portfolio. Evaluates your project descriptions, visual presentation, and how well your portfolio positions you for your target clients.

Open Portfolio Reviewer

Client Red Flag Detector Pro

Paste a job posting or client message and the AI identifies potential red flags — unrealistic expectations, scope creep risks, payment concerns, and communication issues.

Open Red Flag Detector
Calculators

Rate Calculator Free

Calculate your optimal hourly rate based on your skill, experience, country, and market data. Compares your rate against market benchmarks and suggests adjustments.

How to use: Select your skill category, experience level, and country. The calculator shows you market low, average, and high rates with a recommended range.

Open Rate Calculator

Fee Calculator Free

Compare platform fees across Upwork, Fiverr, Freelancer.com, Toptal, and direct payment methods. See exactly how much you keep after fees for any project amount.

Open Fee Calculator
Document Generators

Proposal Writer Pro

Generate client-ready proposals in seconds. Paste a job description and the AI creates a personalized proposal with an attention-grabbing opening, relevant experience highlights, and a clear call to action.

Open Proposal Writer

Invoice Generator Pro

Create professional invoices with your details, client info, line items, taxes, and payment terms. Download as PDF or copy to clipboard.

Open Invoice Generator

SOW Generator Pro

Create detailed Statements of Work from a project description. Includes scope, deliverables, timeline, milestones, acceptance criteria, and payment schedule.

Open SOW Generator

Project Brief Creator Pro

Generate structured project briefs that clearly define objectives, requirements, constraints, and success criteria. Useful for aligning with clients before starting work.

Open Project Brief Creator
Review & Writing

Contract Reviewer Free

Paste a freelance contract and the AI highlights concerning clauses, missing protections, and suggests improvements. Covers payment terms, IP rights, liability, and termination clauses.

Open Contract Reviewer

Scope Analyzer Free

Analyze a project description for scope creep risks. Identifies vague requirements, unrealistic timelines, and missing details that could lead to disputes.

Open Scope Analyzer

Email Writer Pro

Craft professional client emails — follow-ups, rate negotiations, project updates, milestone notifications, and more. Choose a tone and context, and the AI writes it for you.

Open Email Writer
Utilities

Payment Checker Free

Check and compare payment methods available in your country. See processing times, fees, and supported currencies for each option.

Open Payment Checker

Templates Library Free

Browse ready-to-use templates for proposals, contracts, invoices, emails, and more. Copy and customize for your needs.

Open Templates

AI Chat Advisor Pro

Your personal freelance business advisor. Ask about pricing strategies, client management, contract negotiation, tax optimization, or any freelancing question.

Open AI Chat
