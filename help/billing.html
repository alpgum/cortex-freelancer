Payment Methods

We accept all major credit and debit cards through Stripe:

  • Visa, Mastercard, American Express
  • All payments are processed in USD
  • PCI-DSS compliant — we never store your card details

Managing Your Subscription

How to upgrade to Pro

Click the "Upgrade" button in the app or visit the pricing page. You'll be redirected to Stripe's secure checkout. Your Pro access activates immediately after payment.

How to cancel

You can cancel anytime:

  1. Go to your account settings
  2. Click "Manage Billing" to open the Stripe billing portal
  3. Click "Cancel Plan"

After cancellation, your Pro access continues until the end of the current billing period. You won't be charged again.

How to update your payment method

Open the billing portal from your account settings. You can update your card, view past invoices, and download receipts.

Refund Policy

We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee. If you're not satisfied with Pro within 14 days of your first payment, contact us for a full refund.

  • Refunds are processed within 5–10 business days
  • Refunds apply to the most recent charge only
  • After 14 days, partial refunds are not available — cancel to prevent future charges

For full details, see our Refund Policy.

Failed Payment?

If a payment fails, we'll retry automatically over the next few days and send you an email notification. Update your card in the billing portal to resolve the issue. If your subscription lapses, you can re-subscribe anytime without losing your data.

Coupons & Discounts

We occasionally offer promotional codes and discounts. To apply a coupon:

  1. Go to the pricing page
  2. Enter your coupon code at checkout
  3. The discount is applied immediately

Coupons cannot be combined and have expiration dates. Lifetime deal purchases are separate from subscription billing.

Invoices & Receipts

Stripe automatically generates invoices for each payment. You can access them from the billing portal in your account settings. All invoices include your payment amount, date, and can be used for tax purposes.
