## First Comment (by Maker)

Hey Product Hunt!

I'm Alp, and I built Cortex Freelancer because I watched too many talented freelancers spend more time running their business than doing actual work.

The problem is simple: freelancers spend 40-60% of their time on non-billable tasks — job hunting, writing proposals, setting rates, managing payments. That's not sustainable.

So I built 8 specialized AI agents to handle all of it:

1. **Profile Optimizer** — analyzes and improves your freelancer profiles
2. **Job Matcher** — finds relevant listings based on your actual skills
3. **Proposal Writer** — generates personalized proposals (not generic templates)
4. **Rate Strategist** — data-driven pricing recommendations
5. **Client Communicator** — professional responses and follow-ups
6. **Invoice & Payment** — optimizes your payment pathway to cut fees
7. **Review Manager** — helps maintain and improve your ratings
8. **Growth Planner** — weekly action plans tailored to your goals

**Start here:** Try the free profile analyzer — paste your Upwork URL and get an instant profile score, job matches, and payment fee breakdown. No signup needed.

We're focused on freelancers in emerging markets (Egypt, Pakistan, Nigeria, Turkey) where USD earnings matter most and tools are weakest — but this works for every freelancer globally.

Pro tier is $29/month — less than what most freelancers pay in a single wire transfer fee.

I'd love your feedback. What would make this more useful? What am I missing?
