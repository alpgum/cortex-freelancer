# Press Kit — Cortex Freelancer

---

## One-Paragraph Description

Cortex Freelancer is an AI-powered business manager for freelancers. It deploys 8 specialized AI agents to handle the non-billable work that consumes 40-60% of a freelancer's time — profile optimization, job matching, proposal writing, rate strategy, client communication, payment optimization, review management, and growth planning. The platform includes 78 ready-to-use templates, 17 automation scripts, and 6 skill libraries. A free profile analyzer lets any freelancer paste their Upwork URL and get an instant profile score, job matches, and payment fee breakdown with no signup required. Cortex Freelancer is built for the global freelance market with a focus on emerging economies — Egypt, Pakistan, Nigeria, and Turkey — where USD earnings carry the most impact and existing tools fall short.

## The Problem

Freelancers spend 40-60% of their working hours on tasks that don't generate income: searching for clients, writing proposals, optimizing profiles, negotiating rates, managing payments, and handling admin. In emerging markets, this problem is compounded by payment fees that can consume 15-20% of gross earnings through platform cuts, wire transfer fees, and currency conversion spreads. Existing freelancer tools focus on time tracking and invoicing — nobody is automating the business development side.

---

## The Solution

Cortex Freelancer provides 8 AI agents that operate as a freelancer's virtual business team:

1. **Profile Optimizer** — analyzes and improves freelancer profiles for maximum visibility
2. **Job Matcher** — surfaces relevant listings based on skills and work history
3. **Proposal Writer** — generates customized proposals for each opportunity
4. **Rate Strategist** — recommends pricing based on market data and positioning
5. **Client Communicator** — drafts professional responses, follow-ups, and negotiations
6. **Invoice & Payment** — optimizes payment routing to minimize fees
7. **Review Manager** — maintains high ratings and manages client feedback
8. **Growth Planner** — delivers weekly personalized action plans
