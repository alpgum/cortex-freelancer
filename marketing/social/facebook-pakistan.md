I've been building Cortex — an AI business manager for freelancers — and our data from Pakistani users tells an important story:

**The numbers:**

- Average Pakistani freelancer on Upwork: $8-15/hr
- Market rate for same skills internationally: $20-45/hr
- Gap: 40-65% below market value
- Average monthly loss to payment fees: $80-200 (PayPal, Payoneer, wire transfers)
- Hours spent on admin per week: 12-18 (proposals, invoices, emails, scheduling)

**The pattern we see:**

Pakistani freelancers are competing on price when they should be competing on value. The race to the bottom hurts everyone — it pushes rates down for the entire market and teaches clients to expect premium work at budget prices.

**What top Pakistani freelancers do differently:**

1. They benchmark rates against international markets, not local competitors
2. They write proposals that focus on client outcomes, not their hourly rate
3. They have professional contracts, invoices, and communication systems
4. They optimize payment methods to keep more of what they earn
