**Title:** Show HN: Cortex – AI business manager for freelancers (free tools, no signup)

**Body:**

Hey HN,

I built Cortex to solve the admin overhead that eats into freelancer earnings. The core insight: skilled freelancers spend 30-40% of their time on unpaid work (proposals, invoicing, contracts, emails, scheduling).

**What it does:**

6 free tools (no signup, no paywall):
- Rate Calculator — benchmarks your rate against market data by skill/experience/location
- Proposal Generator — AI drafts tailored proposals from job descriptions
- Invoice Creator — multi-currency invoices with payment tracking
- Contract Builder — generates NDAs, scope agreements, freelance contracts
- Email Writer — professional client communication templates
- Scope Analyzer — analyzes project briefs for timeline/risk/gaps

Pro tier ($29/mo) adds 8 AI agents that automate the full workflow: lead generation, proposal sending, project management, invoice chasing, schedule management, contract handling, portfolio optimization, and growth strategy.

**Stats:**
- 2,400+ users, 50+ countries
- 15,000+ analyses processed
- 4.9/5 rating

**Stack:**
- Backend: Node.js / Express
- Auth + data: Firebase
- Payments: Stripe
- Hosting: Vercel
- AI: Custom prompts + function calling

**Why I built this:**

I watched friends in emerging markets (Egypt, Turkey, Nigeria, Pakistan) lose significant income to three things: underpricing (30-50% below market), payment fees (5-10% per transaction), and admin overhead (12-18 hrs/week unpaid). Most freelancer tools are built for US/EU markets and ignore these problems.

**What I'd love feedback on:**

1. The free tools — are they genuinely useful or too basic?
2. Pricing at $29/mo — too high for the target market?
3. The AI agent approach vs. traditional SaaS features
4. Anything else that stands out

Happy to answer questions about the tech, the market, or the business model.
