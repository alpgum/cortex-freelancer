# Go-To-Market Strategy — Cortex Freelancer

---

## Executive Summary

Launch Cortex Freelancer in 3 waves over 3 weeks. The free profile analyzer is the primary acquisition engine — every channel, every post drives to it. Convert engaged free users to Pro ($29/mo) through demonstrated value, not sales pressure.

**Core positioning:** 85% less busywork. 100% more earning.

**Primary hook:** Free Upwork profile analyzer (no signup) → instant score + job matches + fee breakdown.

**Target markets:** Egypt, Pakistan, Nigeria, Turkey (primary), global (secondary).

## Viral Mechanics

### The Free Analyzer Loop

```
[Discovery] → [Try Free Analyzer] → [Get Score + Results] → [Share Score] → [Friends Try It] → [Loop]
```

**Why it works:**

1. **Zero friction:** No signup, no email, no credit card. Just paste a URL.
2. **Shareable output:** "I got a 72 — what's your Freelancer Score?" is inherently shareable.
3. **Emotional trigger:** "You're losing $4,800/year in fees" makes people react and share.
4. **Social proof built-in:** When people share their scores, it's free advertising.
5. **Natural upgrade path:** Free analyzer shows the problem → Pro agents solve it.

### Built-In Viral Features

- [ ] **Twitter share button** on results page ("Share your Freelancer Score")
- [ ] **Score card image** auto-generated for social sharing (like Spotify Wrapped)
- [ ] **Comparison prompt:** "Your score is higher than 65% of freelancers in your niche"
- [ ] **Fee shock value:** Bold display of annual fee loss to trigger screenshots
- [ ] **Referral program:** "Give Pro to a friend, get a free month" (Phase 2)

### Content Viral Loops

- **Fee comparison posts** → emotional reaction → shares → analyzer signups
- **Score reveals** → curiosity → "what's MY score?" → analyzer signups
- **Before/after profiles** → aspiration → "how do I do this?" → analyzer signups
- **Market-specific content** (EGP/PKR/NGN/TRY angles) → local community sharing

## Conversion Funnel

```
Free Analyzer (1,000 users)
  → Engaged users who check results (700 — 70%)
    → Users who return or share (210 — 30% of engaged)
      → Email signups / waitlist (105 — 50% of returners)
        → Pro trial starts (42 — 40% of email)
          → Paid conversion (21 — 50% of trial)
```

**Expected conversion:** ~2% of free analyzer users → paid Pro subscriber.

At 2,000 free users → ~40 Pro subscribers → $1,160/mo MRR.
At 10,000 free users → ~200 Pro subscribers → $5,800/mo MRR.

## Risk Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Reddit post removed (self-promotion) | Lead with value, ask for feedback, don't link in title |
| Product Hunt flop | Diversify — PH is one channel, not the only channel |
| Low conversion to Pro | Extend free trial, add more value to free tier, survey dropoffs |
| Analyzer can't handle traffic | Load test before launch, Vercel auto-scales |
| Negative feedback on analysis quality | Iterate fast, be transparent about v1, use feedback to improve |
| Regional payment barriers | Cenoa handles local currency; also accept crypto |
| Competitor launches similar tool | Speed advantage — already live. Focus on community, not features |
