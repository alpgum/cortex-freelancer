**Cortex Freelancer** gives you three AI agents that handle the business side of freelancing:

🔍 **Business Dev Agent**
- Scans Upwork/Fiverr jobs daily and finds ones that actually match your skills and rate
- Drafts personalized proposals — not templates, actual tailored pitches
- Learns from which proposals win and adjusts over time
- Result: fewer proposals, higher quality, better conversion

📋 **Project Manager Agent**
- Tracks all your projects, deadlines, milestones in one place
- Auto-generates client status updates

💰 **Finance Manager Agent**
- Auto-generates invoices when milestones are complete
- Recommends the cheapest payment method for each client

**For Pakistani freelancers specifically:**

We all know the payment situation is... complicated. JazzCash and EasyPaisa have limits. Bank transfers from international clients can be slow and expensive. Payoneer works but the fees add up.

The Finance Agent compares available payment corridors and recommends the lowest-fee option for each client. If you're getting paid by a US client, it's a different recommendation than a UAE or UK client. It optimizes per transaction so you keep more of what you earn.

**Also worth noting:** Pakistan's IT freelancer tax exemption (valid through 2025 and expected to extend) means your freelance income from IT services has favorable tax treatment. The Finance Agent helps you keep your records clean for that — proper invoices, payment tracking, clear documentation.
