# Podcast Pitch Template

**Purpose:** Secure guest appearances on podcasts relevant to freelancing, solopreneurship, AI, and the future of work.

---

## Target Podcast Categories

1. **Freelancing & Solopreneurship** — Freelance to Founder, The Freelance Podclass, Being Freelance, High-Income Business Writing
2. **SaaS & Startups** — Indie Hackers, My First Million, Startups for the Rest of Us, MicroConf
3. **Future of Work & AI** — The AI Podcast, Practical AI, Future of Work, WorkLife with Adam Grant
4. **Emerging Markets & Global Business** — The Flip (Africa), Afrobility, Startup Middle East, Tech in Asia

## Pitch Template: Freelancing Podcasts

**Subject:** Guest pitch: The data behind why freelancers undercharge (and how AI fixes it)

**Body:**

Hi [Host Name],

I'm [Name], founder of Cortex — an AI business manager used by 2,400+ freelancers across 50+ countries.

We've analyzed 15,000+ freelancer profiles and found some surprising patterns about pricing, proposals, and the admin trap that I think your audience would find valuable.

**3 talking points I can bring:**

1. **The undercharging epidemic** — Our data shows the average freelancer charges 30-50% below market rate. I can share why this happens and the specific steps to fix it (with data, not generic advice).

2. **The 12-18 hour admin tax** — Freelancers lose 12-18 hours per week to unpaid admin work. I'll break down exactly where the time goes and what the highest earners do differently.

3. **AI as a business partner, not a replacement** — How AI agents are handling the admin side of freelancing (proposals, invoicing, client comms) while freelancers focus on what they're actually good at.

**Why this isn't a product pitch:**

I'll focus on actionable insights from our data. The product is context, not the point. Your listeners will walk away with specific strategies they can implement immediately, whether or not they use any tool.

**About me:**
- Built Cortex from zero to 2,400+ users across 50+ countries
- Focused on emerging markets where freelancer tools are scarce
- Background in [relevant founder background]

**Logistics:** I'm flexible on timing, have a professional mic setup, and can record remotely. Happy to do a pre-call to align on topics.

Worth a conversation?

1. **Why we give away 6 tools for free (no signup)** — and how it drives better growth than any paid channel. Our free-to-paid conversion mechanics and the math behind freemium for a $29/mo product.

**Quick stats:**

- Stack: Node.js, Firebase, Stripe, Vercel

Happy to dive deep on any of these. I can do a pre-call to find the angle that fits your show best.

## Follow-Up Framework

**If no response after 7 days:**

Subject: Re: [original subject] — one quick follow-up

"Hi [Name], just bumping this in case it got buried. Happy to adjust the angle to fit your show better — let me know what topics resonate most with your audience."

**If no response after 14 days:**

Move on. Do not send a third follow-up. Add to "revisit in 3 months" list with new angle/data.

**If they respond positively:**

1. Schedule a 15-minute pre-call to align on topics
2. Send a one-page guest brief (bio, talking points, links)
3. Confirm recording logistics (mic, platform, timezone)
4. After recording: send a thank-you note + offer to share the episode with our audience
5. Promote the episode across all Cortex social channels

---

## Preparation Checklist

- [ ] Research the podcast: listen to 2-3 recent episodes
- [ ] Note the host's interview style and preferred topics
- [ ] Prepare 3-5 data points or stories relevant to their audience
- [ ] Have specific, actionable takeaways ready (not just product features)
- [ ] Test mic and recording setup
- [ ] Prepare a special offer for podcast listeners (e.g., extended trial, discount code)
- [ ] Have the landing page URL ready with podcast-specific UTM tracking
