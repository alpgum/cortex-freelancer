# Cortex Freelancer — Promo Video v2 Storyboard

**Format:** 9:16 vertical (1080×1920) — Shorts / Reels / TikTok
**Total Duration:** ~27s (variable shot lengths for rhythm)
**Style:** Cinematic, warm-moody, tech-forward, aspirational
**Story Arc:** Hook → Tension → Magic → Payoff → CTA

---

## Visual Continuity Bible

All clips feature the **same person and environment**:

- **Person:** Male, mid-20s, medium brown skin, short dark curly hair, light stubble. Wearing a fitted black crewneck t-shirt. Lean build.
- **Room:** Modern minimalist home office. Dark walls (charcoal). One large 27" monitor + silver MacBook on a clean oak desk. Black ergonomic mesh chair.
- **Lighting:** Key light = warm amber desk lamp (camera-left). Fill = cool blue-white monitor glow. Background = subtle LED strip (soft purple/blue). Creates cinematic two-tone look.
- **Camera:** Shot on 35mm equivalent, shallow depth of field (f/1.8), subtle film grain, 24fps cinematic feel.

---

## Shot List

### Clip 1 — "The Scroll-Stopper Hook" (4s)

**Camera:** Medium close-up, eye level, slightly off-center (rule of thirds)
**Action:** Freelancer is leaning back in chair, hands behind head, staring at ceiling with a tired/frustrated expression. He slowly looks directly into camera with a "you know the feeling" look.
**Mood:** Relatable frustration — every freelancer's been here
**Text Overlay:** **"Tired of chasing clients?"** (appears at 0.5s, bottom-center)
**Music:** Lo-fi beat fades in, mellow and slightly melancholic
**Psychology:** Opens with pain point. Direct eye contact = scroll-stopper. Viewer thinks "that's me."

### Clip 2 — "AI Takes Over" (6s)

**Camera:** Over-the-shoulder, looking at the monitor screen. Rack focus from person's shoulder (blurred) to screen (sharp).
**Action:** The monitor shows a job marketplace interface. Multiple job listings appear. A glowing green selection highlight automatically moves down the list, pausing on a high-paying gig ($3,500 — Web App). The AI is scanning and selecting.
**Mood:** Futuristic, automated, satisfying to watch
**Text Overlay:** **"AI scans. AI matches."** (appears at 1s)
**Music:** Beat subtly picks up, adds a synth layer
**Psychology:** Tension builds — "wait, is this real?" The automation feels magical.

### Clip 3 — "Ghost Apply" (5s)

**Camera:** Tight close-up of keyboard and lower screen. Dutch angle (slight tilt) for dynamism.
**Action:** Keys press themselves — ghost typing at superhuman speed. The screen shows a proposal form being auto-filled with perfectly tailored text. Words appear faster than humanly possible.
**Mood:** Hypnotic, fast, impressive
**Text Overlay:** **"Applies while you sleep."** (appears at 1s)
**Music:** Beat intensifies, rapid hi-hats
**Psychology:** The "wow" factor peaks. This is the magic moment.

### Clip 4 — "The Win" (6s)

**Camera:** Close-up of the freelancer's face, lit by green screen glow. Camera slowly pushes in (dolly zoom feel).
**Action:** His eyes widen. A bright green notification reflects in his eyes. His expression transforms from neutral → surprise → pure joy. He sits up straight, leans forward.
**Mood:** Triumphant, emotional, the payoff
**Text Overlay:** **"You're hired. $3,500."** (green text, appears at 1.5s)
**SFX:** Notification DING at 0.5s
**Music:** Bass drop, then resolves into warm chords
**Psychology:** The emotional climax. Viewer feels the dopamine hit vicariously.

### Clip 5 — "CTA + Brand" (6s)

**Camera:** Medium shot, straight-on, centered. Freelancer in full frame at desk.
**Action:** He breaks into a big grin, shakes his head in disbelief, then gestures toward the camera with a "you should try this" hand motion. Confident, warm energy.
**Mood:** Inviting, aspirational, warm
**Text Overlay:** **"cortexfreelancer.com"** (white, bold, center — appears at 1s, stays until end)
**Music:** Beat resolves, warm fade out
**Psychology:** Social proof through genuine reaction. Hand gesture = invitation. URL burns into memory via hold time.

## Editing Notes

- **Transitions:** 0.5s crossfade between all clips
- **Fade-in:** 0.5s from black at start
- **Fade-out:** 1.0s to black at end
- **Text style:** White bold sans-serif, 64px, with black drop shadow (3px offset). Green for Clip 4 text.
- **Text position:** Center-bottom, ~200px from bottom edge
- **Audio layers:** Lo-fi beat (continuous), notification ding (Clip 4 @ 0.5s)
- **Total runtime:** 4 + 6 + 5 + 6 + 6 = 27s (+ transitions ≈ 25-26s effective)
