- **Visual:** Green notification popup on screen — "Congratulations". Confetti. Green glow on face.

- **Visual:** Freelancer still has hands in air, goes from relaxed → shocked → amazed. Mouth drops.
- **Text Overlay:** *"cortexfreelancer.com"*
