# Influencer Outreach — Cortex Freelancer

---

## Target Influencer Types

### 1. Upwork YouTubers
People who make content about succeeding on Upwork. They have audiences of freelancers actively looking for optimization tips.

**Examples to find:** Search YouTube for "Upwork tips", "how to get clients on Upwork", "Upwork profile optimization"

### 2. Freelancer Twitter/X Accounts
Accounts that tweet about freelancing, remote work, and making money online. Often have 5K-50K followers of aspiring and active freelancers.

**Examples to find:** Search X for "freelance tips", check who popular freelancers follow

### 3. Productivity/Tool Reviewers
YouTubers and bloggers who review SaaS tools, productivity apps, and AI products. They're always looking for new products to feature.

**Examples to find:** Search "best freelancer tools 2026", "AI productivity tools review"

### 4. Digital Nomad Influencers
People who document remote work lifestyles. Their audiences are freelancers and remote workers who would benefit from Cortex.

**Examples to find:** Search Instagram/YouTube for "digital nomad", "remote work lifestyle"

### 5. Freelance Course Creators
People who sell courses or coaching on freelancing. They could recommend Cortex as a complementary tool.

**Examples to find:** Search Udemy, Skillshare for freelancing course instructors

### 6. Regional Tech Influencers (EG/PK/NG/TR)
Tech content creators in our target markets who cover tools, earning online, and the local tech scene.

**Examples to find:** Search YouTube in Arabic, Urdu, Turkish for freelancing content

### 7. LinkedIn Thought Leaders (Future of Work)
People posting regularly about remote work, freelancing trends, and the gig economy. Usually 10K-100K followers.

**Examples to find:** Search LinkedIn for "future of work", "freelance economy" posts with high engagement

### 8. Podcast Hosts (Freelancing/SaaS)
Hosts of podcasts about freelancing, solopreneurship, or SaaS building. A single episode can drive hundreds of signups.

**Examples to find:** Search Apple Podcasts/Spotify for "freelancing", "solopreneur"

### 9. Community Leaders (Facebook/Discord/Slack)
Admins of large freelancer communities. They have direct influence over engaged audiences.

**Examples to find:** Look at admins of top freelancer Facebook groups and Discord servers

### 10. AI/No-Code Tool Advocates
People who cover AI tools, no-code solutions, and automation. They'll be interested in the AI agent angle.

**Examples to find:** Search for "AI tools for business", "no-code automation" content creators
