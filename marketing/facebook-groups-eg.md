Hey everyone 👋

Quick question for the group — how much time do you spend every week on the "business side" of freelancing? Not the actual work, but finding jobs, writing proposals, invoicing clients, figuring out how to get paid without losing a chunk to fees?

I tracked mine. It was almost 18 hours a week. Almost half my working time on stuff that doesn't earn directly.

So I built something to fix it.

**Cortex Freelancer** — an AI-powered business manager for freelancers. It's three AI agents:

🔍 **Business Dev Agent** — Finds jobs that match your skills on Upwork and other platforms, then drafts personalized proposals for you. You review and send. What takes 2 hours becomes 20 minutes.

📋 **Project Manager Agent** — Tracks your deadlines across all clients, writes status updates, flags problems before they blow up.

💰 **Finance Manager Agent** — This one is big for us in Egypt. It generates invoices, but more importantly, it analyzes payment methods and recommends the cheapest option for each client's country.

**On the payment thing specifically:**

We all know the pain. You finish a project, invoice $1,000, and by the time it hits your account you're looking at $950 or less depending on the method. Multiply that across a year and you're losing serious money.

The Finance Agent compares options — bank wires, Payoneer, newer platforms — and tells you which one has the lowest fees for that specific corridor. Egypt → US client? Different answer than Egypt → Gulf client. It optimizes per transaction.
