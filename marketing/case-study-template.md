## Guidelines for Case Study Creation

### Sourcing candidates
- Identify users with strong before/after metrics in the analytics dashboard
- Look for users who've been active for 2+ months (enough time for meaningful results)
- Prioritize diverse representation: different countries, skills, experience levels
- Ask customer support to flag users who've sent positive feedback

### Interview questions
1. What were you doing before Cortex? Walk me through a typical week.
2. What was the most frustrating part of running your freelance business?
3. How did you first hear about Cortex? What made you try it?
4. Which tool or feature did you try first? What happened?
5. Can you share specific numbers — your rate before and after, time saved, fees reduced?
6. What surprised you most about using Cortex?
7. Would you recommend Cortex to other freelancers? What would you tell them?
8. What would you improve about the product?

### Writing guidelines
- Lead with the result in the headline — make it specific and quantifiable
- Use the freelancer's own words wherever possible (direct quotes)
- Include specific numbers — vague claims ("saved a lot of time") are weak
- Be honest about the timeline — results don't happen overnight
- Include the Cortex cost vs. ROI calculation explicitly
- Keep it under 1,200 words — respect the reader's time
- End with a clear, relevant CTA

### Distribution
- Publish on blog/website
- Create social media snippets (pull key stats + quotes)
- Include in email sequences (especially Email 4 of welcome sequence)
- Share in relevant community groups (with permission)
- Use in sales conversations and landing pages
