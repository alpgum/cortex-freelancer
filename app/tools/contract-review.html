The Problem

Contracts are written to protect the client

Most freelancers sign without reading. The ones who read don't know what to look for.

⚠️

"Work for hire" traps

You build it, they own it -- including your portfolio rights, future derivatives, and reusable code.

💰

Missing payment terms

No milestones, no late fees, no kill fee. You do the work and hope they pay.

🔓

Scope creep loopholes

Vague deliverables and "unlimited revisions" let clients expand the project endlessly.
