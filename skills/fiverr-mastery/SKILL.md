# Fiverr Mastery — Skill Library

## How Fiverr Search Algorithm Works

### Ranking Factors (Approximate Weight)
1. **Relevance:** Gig title, tags, and description match the buyer's search query. Keywords are king.
2. **Performance metrics:** Order completion rate, on-time delivery, response time, cancellation rate
3. **Seller level:** New → Level 1 → Level 2 → Top Rated. Higher levels get more visibility.
4. **Click-through rate:** If your gig thumbnail and title get more clicks, Fiverr shows it more.
5. **Conversion rate:** If buyers who view your gig actually order, Fiverr rewards with more impressions.
6. **Recency:** Recently active gigs (updated, orders in progress) rank above dormant gigs.
7. **Response time:** Under 1 hour = maximum boost. Over 24 hours = ranking penalty.

### Gig Optimization Formula

**Title (80 chars max):**
- Formula: "I will [ACTION] [SPECIFIC DELIVERABLE] for [AUDIENCE/PURPOSE]"
- Front-load the main keyword (first 3-4 words get the most search weight)
- Include outcome language ("that converts," "that ranks," "that sells")

**Tags (5 slots):**
- Slot 1: Exact match of your primary keyword ("shopify store design")
- Slot 2: Close variant ("ecommerce website design")
- Slot 3: Broader category ("website design")
- Slot 4: Related term ("online store")
- Slot 5: Long-tail niche ("dropshipping website")
- **Research method:** Type your keyword in Fiverr search → note autocomplete → those are high-volume searches

**Description:**
- Para 1: Pain point + promise (hook the buyer)
- Para 2: What's included (bullet points by tier)
- Para 3: Why you (differentiators with proof)
- Para 4: Process (3-4 steps)
- Para 5: FAQ (3-5 questions — also boosts SEO)

**Thumbnail:**
- High-contrast, professional image
- Text overlay with key benefit or deliverable type
- Consistent style across all your gigs
- Mockup or portfolio piece if visual work
- NO stock photos — buyers spot them instantly

### Pricing Psychology — The 3-Tier Strategy

**Basic tier:** "Foot in the door" — low risk for the buyer, gets them into your funnel.
- Price it attractively but not so low that it attracts problem buyers
- Deliver something valuable but minimal
- Goal: Get the first order + review

**Standard tier:** "The sweet spot" — where most orders should land.
- Price at 2-2.5x Basic
- Include the most common scope requests
- Goal: Bread and butter revenue

**Premium tier:** "The full experience" — highest margin.
- Price at 3-5x Basic
- Include everything + fastest delivery + extra revisions
- Goal: Premium clients who don't want to think about tiers

**Gig Extras:**
- Extra-fast delivery: +30-50% of tier price
- Additional revisions: +$20-50 per round
- Source files: +$25-100 (if not included in base)
- Custom extras for your niche: +varies

### Seller Path

**New Seller → Level 1 (60 days):**
- Complete 10+ orders
- Maintain 4.7+ star rating
- 90%+ response rate within 24 hours
- 90%+ order completion rate
- 90%+ on-time delivery

**Level 1 → Level 2 (120 days at Level 1):**
- Complete 50+ orders
- $2,000+ in earnings
- Same quality metrics as Level 1

**Level 2 → Top Rated Seller:**
- By invitation
- 100+ orders, $20,000+ earnings
- Consistently exceptional metrics
- Active for 180+ days

### Review Management
- **After delivery:** Send a polite message thanking them and mentioning you'd appreciate a review
- **Never ask explicitly for 5 stars** — against Fiverr TOS
- **Handling negative reviews:** Respond publicly and professionally. Acknowledge the issue, explain what happened, offer resolution. Future buyers read your responses.
- **Fiverr's review system:** Buyers can update reviews. If you resolve an issue, politely ask if they'd consider updating their review.
