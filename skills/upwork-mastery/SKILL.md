# Upwork Mastery — Skill Library

## How Upwork Search Ranking Works

### The Algorithm's Key Signals (In Order of Weight)
1. **Job Success Score (JSS):** Single most important factor. Minimum 80% to stay competitive. 90%+ for premium visibility.
2. **Earned amount:** Higher lifetime earnings = higher search ranking. The platform rewards proven earners.
3. **Relevance:** Profile title, skills, and overview match the client's search query. Keyword optimization matters.
4. **Response time:** Profiles with <2 hour average response time rank higher in search and get more invitations.
5. **Availability badge:** Profiles marked "Available" get priority over those without the badge.
6. **Recent activity:** Active profiles (recent proposals, messages, contracts) rank above dormant ones.
7. **Specialized profile match:** If you have a specialized profile that matches the category, it ranks higher than a general profile.

### JSS Deep Dive — How It's Really Calculated
JSS is calculated based on a rolling window of your last ~20 contracts (or roughly 24 months).

**What counts positively:**
- Client rates you 4-5 stars in private feedback (heavier weight than public feedback)
- Long-term contracts (12+ weeks) boost JSS more than short gigs
- Repeat clients (multiple contracts with same client) are strong positive signals
- Contracts closed cleanly with both parties satisfied

**What hurts JSS:**
- Client rates you 1-3 stars in private feedback (you may never see this)
- Contracts that end with no feedback (counted as "neutral" but can drag JSS down)
- Refunds and disputes (even partial refunds hurt significantly)
- Multiple short contracts ending quickly (looks like failed relationships)

**Protecting your JSS:**
- Always get the client to leave feedback when closing a contract
- Resolve issues directly with clients before they become disputes
- Avoid very small contracts ($50-100) with new clients — the risk/reward is poor
- If a contract is going badly, communicate early and close with mutual agreement rather than letting it fester

### Connects Strategy
- Each proposal costs 2-6 Connects depending on job value
- Free accounts get 10 Connects/month. Paid (Freelancer Plus) gets 80.
- **When to spend:** Jobs posted <4 hours ago, <10 proposals, verified payment, budget >$500
- **When to save:** Jobs with 20+ proposals, unverified payment, <$100 budget, vague scope
- **Boosted proposals:** Spending extra Connects moves you to the top of the list. Use for high-value ($5K+) jobs where you're a perfect fit.
- **ROI mindset:** If 1 in 10 proposals wins and your average project is $2,000, each proposal has an expected value of $200. A few Connects is a bargain.

### Rising Talent → Top Rated → Top Rated Plus

**Rising Talent (New accounts, first 90 days):**
- Requirements: Complete profile, active proposals, first contract
- Benefits: Badge, slight search boost, credibility for new profiles
- Duration: 90 days (use this window aggressively — it's your best visibility period as a newcomer)

**Top Rated (The standard of excellence):**
- Requirements: $1,000+ earned, 90%+ JSS, 12+ months on platform, no recent account holds
- Benefits: Top Rated badge, exclusive job access, JSS protection (one bad review won't tank you)
- Timeline: Typically 6-18 months to achieve

**Top Rated Plus (The elite tier):**
- Requirements: Top Rated status + sustained high performance + high earnings + long-term client relationships
- Benefits: Dedicated success manager, premium job invitations, highest search priority
- Timeline: Typically 18-36 months

### Specialized Profiles
- You can create up to 2 specialized profiles (in addition to your general one)
- Each has its own title, overview, portfolio, and skills
- **When to use:** When you serve 2+ distinct markets (e.g., "React Developer" and "Technical SEO Consultant")
- **Benefit:** Higher relevance scores because the profile perfectly matches the search category
- **Caution:** Don't spread yourself too thin. 1 strong specialized profile beats 2 weak ones.

### Fee Structure and Pricing
- 10% service fee on first $500 with a client
- 10% on billings $500.01-$10,000
- 5% on billings over $10,000 with the same client
- **Pricing strategy:** Account for the fee in your rate. If you want to net $50/hr, price at $56/hr.
- **Long-term clients:** The fee drops to 5% after $10K — this is a strong argument for retainer relationships.
