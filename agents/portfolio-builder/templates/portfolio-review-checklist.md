# Portfolio Review Checklist

Self-audit your portfolio monthly. Score each item YES/NO.

---

## Profile Basics
- [ ] **Profile photo:** Professional, recent, clear face, friendly expression
- [ ] **Title/headline:** Contains primary keyword + niche + value proposition
- [ ] **Bio/overview:** Opens with a compelling hook (not "I am a passionate...")
- [ ] **Bio includes:** Specific results, metrics, and proof points
- [ ] **Bio has CTA:** Clear call-to-action telling prospects what to do next
- [ ] **Skills listed:** Top 10-15 relevant skills, ordered by priority
- [ ] **Availability status:** Set to available / online (platform-specific)

## Portfolio Items
- [ ] **Minimum 4 items** displayed (aim for 8-12)
- [ ] **Each item has a compelling title** (not just "Website Design")
- [ ] **Each item includes specific results/metrics** (numbers, not adjectives)
- [ ] **Thumbnails are high quality** (device mockups, not raw screenshots)
- [ ] **Best/most relevant work is first** in the display order
- [ ] **No outdated work** (remove anything older than 2 years unless it's exceptional)
- [ ] **Variety shown** (if applicable — different project types, industries)

## Social Proof
- [ ] **5+ reviews/ratings** on primary platform
- [ ] **Average rating above 4.8** stars
- [ ] **At least 2 detailed testimonials** (not just "Great work!")
- [ ] **Testimonials mention specific results** or experiences
- [ ] **LinkedIn recommendations:** At least 3 from clients
- [ ] **Badges/certifications** displayed (Top Rated, Rising Talent, etc.)

## Content & Visibility
- [ ] **Case studies:** At least 2 detailed case studies published
- [ ] **LinkedIn activity:** Posted or commented in the last 7 days
- [ ] **Profile keywords** match what clients search for
- [ ] **Contact info** is easy to find (booking link, email, etc.)

## Competitive Check
- [ ] **Searched for yourself** on primary platform — where do you rank?
- [ ] **Reviewed top 3 competitors** — what are they doing that you're not?
- [ ] **Rate positioning** is clear and justified by portfolio quality
- [ ] **Niche positioning** is apparent within 5 seconds of viewing your profile

---

## Scoring
- **12+ YES:** Your portfolio is strong. Focus on incremental improvements.
- **8-11 YES:** Good foundation but missing key elements. Prioritize the gaps.
- **Below 8:** Significant overhaul needed. Start with profile basics and 4 portfolio items.
