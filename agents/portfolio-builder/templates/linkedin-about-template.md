## Tips
- **First 2 lines are everything** — they determine if someone clicks "See More"
- **Use line breaks and symbols** (→ • ★) to improve scannability
- **Include a CTA** — tell them exactly what to do next
- **Update every quarter** with fresh results and metrics
- **Write in first person** — LinkedIn is personal, not corporate
