# Weekly Content Calendar for Freelancer Authority

## Content Pillars (Pick 3 That Fit Your Niche)

1. **Behind the Scenes** — Show your process, tools, workspace
2. **Lessons & Mistakes** — What you learned the hard way
3. **Client Results** — Case study snippets, metrics, before/after
4. **Tips & Tutorials** — Teach something in your niche
5. **Industry Commentary** — React to trends, news, tools in your field

---

## Weekly Schedule Template

### Monday: Industry Insight
**Format:** Text post (LinkedIn) or thread (Twitter/X)
**Topic ideas:**
- "3 trends I'm seeing in [YOUR_INDUSTRY] this month"
- "Hot take: [CONTROVERSIAL_BUT_DEFENSIBLE_OPINION]"
- React to a recent industry article or tool launch
- "What [BIG_COMPANY] gets right/wrong about [TOPIC]"

### Wednesday: Behind the Scenes / Tips
**Format:** Carousel/images (LinkedIn) or thread (Twitter/X)
**Topic ideas:**
- "My design process in 5 steps (with screenshots)"
- "The tool stack I use for every project"
- "How I [SPECIFIC_TASK] in under [TIME]"
- Before/after of a current project (with client permission)
- "5 mistakes I see [ROLE_PLURAL] make with [TOPIC]"

### Friday: Results / Social Proof
**Format:** Text post with image/metric
**Topic ideas:**
- "Just shipped: [PROJECT_DESCRIPTION] → [RESULT]"
- Client testimonial with your commentary
- Case study snippet: "How we achieved [RESULT] for [CLIENT]"
- Monthly/quarterly recap of your wins
- "What I learned from [SPECIFIC_PROJECT]"

## Posting Tips
- **Consistency > quality:** A good post every week beats a perfect post once a month
- **Engage for 15 min after posting:** Reply to every comment in the first hour
- **Best times:** LinkedIn: Tue-Thu 8-10 AM. Twitter: Mon-Fri 9 AM, 12 PM, 5 PM (client's timezone)
- **Repurpose:** One case study = LinkedIn post + Twitter thread + portfolio update + email newsletter
