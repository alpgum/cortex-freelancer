# Fiverr Gig Template

## Gig Title (80 chars max)
"I will [ACTION_VERB] [SPECIFIC_DELIVERABLE] for [TARGET_AUDIENCE]"

**Examples:**
- "I will design a modern Shopify store that converts visitors to buyers"
- "I will write SEO blog posts that rank on Google for your SaaS company"
- "I will create a professional logo and brand identity for your startup"

**Rules:** Front-load the main keyword. No filler words. Be specific about the outcome.
