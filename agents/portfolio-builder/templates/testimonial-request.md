# Testimonial Request

## Version 1: Quick Ask (Right After Positive Feedback)

Hi [CLIENT_NAME],

So glad to hear you're happy with the results! Would you mind sharing a quick testimonial? It would mean a lot for my portfolio.

Two quick questions (feel free to answer in a couple sentences each):
1. What challenge were you facing before we worked together?
2. What results have you seen since the project was completed?

I'll format it nicely — you just need to approve the final version.

Thanks!
[YOUR_NAME]

---

## Version 2: Offer to Draft It (For Busy Clients)

Hi [CLIENT_NAME],

I'm updating my portfolio and would love to include our [PROJECT_NAME] project. Would you be open to providing a brief testimonial?

To make it super easy, I've drafted something based on our results. Feel free to edit, adjust, or rewrite it entirely:

> "[DRAFT_TESTIMONIAL — e.g., 'Working with [YOUR_NAME] was seamless. They redesigned our landing page and we saw a 35% increase in signups within the first month. Clear communication, on-time delivery, and results that exceeded expectations. Highly recommended.']"

If that captures your experience, just let me know and I'll use it. If you'd prefer to write your own, that's great too!

[YOUR_NAME]

---

## Version 3: Platform-Specific (Upwork/Fiverr Review)

Hi [CLIENT_NAME],

If you have a moment, would you mind leaving a review on [PLATFORM]? It really helps my ranking and visibility on the platform.

Here's the direct link: [REVIEW_LINK]

If you're willing, mentioning [SPECIFIC_THING — e.g., "the conversion results" or "the communication and on-time delivery"] would be especially helpful for future clients evaluating me.

No pressure at all — but if you could, I'd genuinely appreciate it!

[YOUR_NAME]

---

## Tips
- **Ask within 48 hours** of successful delivery (the "glow window")
- **Make it easy:** Draft it for them or ask specific questions
- **Be specific** about what to mention (results, communication, reliability)
- **Platform reviews > written testimonials** for algorithm impact
- **Never incentivize reviews** (against most platform TOS)
- **Follow up once** if they don't respond — then let it go
