# LinkedIn Headline Formulas

Your headline is the most visible text on your profile — it appears in search results, comments, and connection requests. Make it count.

---

## 10 Proven Formulas

### 1. The Outcome Formula
**"I help [TARGET] achieve [OUTCOME] through [METHOD]"**
- "I help SaaS startups increase trial conversions through landing page optimization"
- "I help e-commerce brands grow revenue through high-converting email funnels"

### 2. The Specialist Formula
**"[NICHE] [ROLE] | [PROOF_POINT]"**
- "Shopify Performance Specialist | 200+ stores optimized, avg 40% speed improvement"
- "B2B SaaS Copywriter | $12M+ in pipeline generated through content"

### 3. The Results Formula
**"[ROLE] → [SPECIFIC_RESULT] for [X]+ clients"**
- "UI/UX Designer → Increased user engagement 35% avg for 50+ SaaS products"
- "SEO Consultant → Page 1 rankings for 120+ keywords across 30+ clients"

### 4. The Authority Formula
**"[ROLE] | [PUBLICATION/PLATFORM] | [CREDENTIAL]"**
- "Full-Stack Developer | Top Rated Plus on Upwork | 100% JSS"
- "Brand Designer | Featured in Dribbble | 8 years, 300+ brands"

### 5. The Problem-Solver Formula
**"Fixing [PROBLEM] for [TARGET] | [YOUR_SERVICE]"**
- "Fixing slow websites for e-commerce brands | Performance Engineering"
- "Fixing broken funnels for B2B SaaS | Conversion Rate Optimization"

### 6. The Dual-Skill Formula
**"[SKILL_1] + [SKILL_2] = [UNIQUE_VALUE]"**
- "Data Science + Storytelling = Dashboards that drive decisions"
- "Code + Design = Full-stack products built by one person"

### 7. The Proof-Heavy Formula
**"[X] [METRIC] | [Y] [METRIC] | [ROLE]"**
- "$5M+ revenue generated | 200+ projects | Conversion Copywriter"
- "50+ apps shipped | 4.8★ avg rating | React Native Developer"

### 8. The Niche-Down Formula
**"The [NICHE] [ROLE] | [SUB-NICHE] expert"**
- "The DTC Brand Designer | Packaging & Visual Identity for CPG startups"
- "The Healthcare UX Specialist | Patient portal & telehealth app design"

### 9. The Transformation Formula
**"Turning [INPUT] into [OUTPUT] for [TARGET]"**
- "Turning complex data into clear dashboards for C-suite executives"
- "Turning brand stories into scroll-stopping social content"

### 10. The Simple + Proof Formula
**"[ROLE] | [ONE_KILLER_STAT]"**
- "Freelance Developer | 100% on-time delivery across 150+ projects"
- "Content Strategist | Grew 3 blogs from 0 to 100K monthly visitors"

---

## Rules
- **Max 220 characters** — use them all
- **No job titles alone** — "Freelance Web Developer" tells nothing
- **Include numbers** — they stop the scroll
- **Front-load the value** — lead with what matters to the reader
- **Update quarterly** — refresh with new metrics and positioning
