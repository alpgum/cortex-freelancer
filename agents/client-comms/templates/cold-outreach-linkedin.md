# Cold Outreach — LinkedIn

## Connection Request Note (300 char limit)

Hi [CLIENT_NAME], I came across your post about [TOPIC_THEY_POSTED_ABOUT] — really insightful perspective on [SPECIFIC_POINT]. I work with companies like [THEIR_COMPANY] on [YOUR_SERVICE]. Would love to connect.

## Follow-Up Message (After Connection Accepted)

Hi [CLIENT_NAME],

Thanks for connecting! I enjoyed your recent post about [TOPIC] — especially the point about [SPECIFIC_DETAIL].

I've been following [THEIR_COMPANY]'s work in [THEIR_INDUSTRY], and I noticed [SPECIFIC_OBSERVATION_ABOUT_THEIR_BUSINESS — e.g., "your website's mobile experience could be significantly faster" or "your content strategy is strong but missing video"].

I help [TYPE_OF_COMPANY] with [YOUR_SERVICE] — recently worked with [SIMILAR_COMPANY] and helped them [SPECIFIC_RESULT — e.g., "increase conversion rates by 35%" or "cut their page load time from 6s to 1.2s"].

Would it be worth a quick 15-minute call to see if I could help with [SPECIFIC_AREA]? No pitch — just a conversation.

Either way, great to be connected.

[YOUR_NAME]

## Tips for Success

- **Before connecting:** Like 2-3 of their posts and leave one thoughtful comment over 1-2 weeks
- **Connection note:** Always reference something specific about them — generic notes get ignored
- **Timing:** Send follow-up 24-48 hours after they accept, not immediately
- **Tone:** Peer-to-peer, not salesperson-to-prospect
- **CTA:** Always low-commitment ("quick chat" not "let me send you a proposal")
- **Profile check:** Make sure YOUR LinkedIn profile is optimized before reaching out — they will check it
