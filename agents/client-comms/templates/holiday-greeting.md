# Holiday Greeting

Use for seasonal relationship maintenance — keeps you top of mind without being transactional.

---

## Version 1: End of Year (Universal)

Subject: Wishing you a great [YEAR+1]

Hi [CLIENT_NAME],

As [CURRENT_YEAR] wraps up, I wanted to take a moment to say thank you.

Working with you on [PROJECT_OR_GENERAL — e.g., "the website redesign" or "your projects this year"] has been a genuine highlight. [PERSONAL_TOUCH — e.g., "Your team's creativity makes every project fun" or "I've learned a lot about your industry through our collaboration"].

Wishing you and the [THEIR_COMPANY] team a wonderful holiday season and a [YEAR+1] full of [RELEVANT_WISH — e.g., "growth and great projects" or "new milestones"].

Looking forward to continuing our work together in the new year. If you're planning anything exciting for Q1, I'd love to hear about it.

Happy holidays!

[YOUR_NAME]

---

## Version 2: Eid Greeting (MENA / South Asian Clients)

Subject: Eid Mubarak!

Dear [CLIENT_NAME],

Eid Mubarak to you and your family! Wishing you a blessed and joyful celebration.

Thank you for our wonderful working relationship — it's been a pleasure collaborating with you on [PROJECT/GENERAL]. I hope the holiday brings you rest, happiness, and time with loved ones.

Looking forward to reconnecting after the holiday.

Warm regards,
[YOUR_NAME]

## Version 3: Ramadan Greeting

Subject: Ramadan Kareem

Dear [CLIENT_NAME],

Ramadan Kareem! Wishing you a peaceful and blessed month.

Just a heads up — I'll be adjusting my working hours during Ramadan to [ADJUSTED_HOURS]. I'll still be fully available for [THEIR_PROJECT] and will respond to messages within [TIMEFRAME].

If your schedule is also shifting during this time, I'm happy to adjust our meeting times and deadlines accordingly.

Warm regards,
[YOUR_NAME]

---

## Version 4: Quick Thank-You (Any Occasion)

Subject: Quick thank you

Hi [CLIENT_NAME],

No agenda here — just wanted to say I appreciate working with you. [SPECIFIC_REASON — e.g., "You give clear feedback, you respect timelines, and you're genuinely great to collaborate with. That's rarer than you'd think."]

Here's to more great work together.

[YOUR_NAME]

---

## Cultural Calendar Awareness
- **Western:** Christmas/New Year (Dec 25 - Jan 1), Thanksgiving (US, late Nov)
- **MENA:** Eid al-Fitr, Eid al-Adha, Ramadan (dates shift yearly — check the Islamic calendar)
- **South Asian:** Diwali (Oct/Nov), Eid
- **East Asian:** Lunar New Year (Jan/Feb)
- **Universal:** New Year (Jan 1) is safe for any client

## Tips
- Keep it short and genuine — this isn't a sales pitch
- Send 1-2 days before the holiday, not the day of
- Match the tone to your relationship — formal for new clients, warm for long-term ones
- Never combine a holiday greeting with a business request or invoice
