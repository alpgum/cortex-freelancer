# Ask for Referral

Use after a successful project when the client is happy with your work.

---

## Version 1: Direct Ask (Post-Delivery Glow)

Subject: Quick favor?

Hi [CLIENT_NAME],

I'm really glad you're happy with [PROJECT_NAME] — it was a great project to work on.

I have a quick ask: do you know anyone in your network who might need [YOUR_SERVICE]? I'm taking on [NUMBER] new clients this quarter and I find that my best projects come from referrals.

No pressure at all — but if someone comes to mind, I'd be grateful for an introduction. And of course, if there's anything I can do for you in return, just say the word.

Thanks for being such a great client to work with.

[YOUR_NAME]

---

## Version 2: Incentivized Referral

Subject: A thank-you offer for you

Hi [CLIENT_NAME],

Working on [PROJECT_NAME] was a highlight of my [MONTH/QUARTER]. I'm currently looking to grow my client base through referrals, and I'd love your help.

Here's what I'm offering: **for every client you refer who books a project, I'll give you [INCENTIVE — e.g., "$200 credit toward future work" or "a free hour of consulting" or "10% off your next project"].**

If anyone in your network needs [YOUR_SERVICE — be specific], feel free to share my info:
- Portfolio: [PORTFOLIO_LINK]
- Email: [YOUR_EMAIL]
- Or I'm happy to be introduced directly via email

Either way, thank you for the great collaboration. Looking forward to working together again.

[YOUR_NAME]

---

## Version 3: Testimonial + Referral Combo

Subject: Would you say a few words about our work?

Hi [CLIENT_NAME],

I'm updating my portfolio and would love to include our [PROJECT_NAME] project. Would you be open to:

1. **A brief testimonial** — 2-3 sentences about the experience. I can even draft something for you to edit if that's easier. (For my [PLATFORM — Upwork/LinkedIn/website].)

2. **A referral** — if you know anyone who needs [YOUR_SERVICE], I'd be grateful for an intro.

No obligation on either — but both would mean a lot.

Thanks again for a great project!

[YOUR_NAME]

---

## Timing
- **Best time:** 1-3 days after successful delivery when satisfaction is highest
- **Also good:** After they share positive results ("Our traffic doubled!")
- **After providing extra value:** When you've gone above and beyond, goodwill is at peak
- **Never ask:** During unresolved issues, during scope disputes, or when they seem stressed
