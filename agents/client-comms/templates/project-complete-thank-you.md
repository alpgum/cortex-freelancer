# Project Complete — Thank You

Use when wrapping up a project to leave a lasting positive impression.

---

## Standard Version

Subject: [PROJECT_NAME] — delivered + thank you

Hi [CLIENT_NAME],

[PROJECT_NAME] is officially wrapped! Here's a summary of what we accomplished:

**Deliverables:**
- [DELIVERABLE_1 — e.g., "Fully responsive 8-page website (desktop + mobile)"]
- [DELIVERABLE_2 — e.g., "Custom CMS setup with admin training documentation"]
- [DELIVERABLE_3 — e.g., "SEO optimization for 15 target keywords"]

**Key Results:**
- [RESULT_1 — e.g., "Page load time: 1.4 seconds (down from 5.8s)"]
- [RESULT_2 — e.g., "Mobile score: 94/100 on Google PageSpeed"]

**Files & Access:**
- All source files: [LINK_OR_LOCATION]
- Credentials/access: [WHERE_THEY_CAN_FIND_THEM]
- Documentation: [LINK_IF_APPLICABLE]

**Post-delivery support:** I'll be available for [SUPPORT_PERIOD — e.g., "2 weeks of bug fixes"] after delivery at no additional charge. After that, I'm always here if you need help.

It's been a pleasure working with you. Your [SPECIFIC_COMPLIMENT — e.g., "clear vision and quick feedback made this project a dream to work on"].

Three quick asks before I sign off:

1. **Review:** If you're happy with the work, a [PLATFORM] review would mean the world to me. [REVIEW_LINK_IF_APPLICABLE]
2. **Referrals:** If anyone in your network needs [YOUR_SERVICE], I'd appreciate an introduction.
3. **Future work:** I'd love to help with [NEXT_OPPORTUNITY — e.g., "the Phase 2 features we discussed" or "ongoing maintenance"]. Just say the word.

Thank you for trusting me with this project. Wishing [THEIR_COMPANY] all the best!

[YOUR_NAME]

---

## Tips
- **Send within 24 hours** of final delivery acceptance
- **Be specific** about what was delivered — it reinforces the value
- **Include metrics** if possible — numbers make results tangible
- **The triple ask** (review, referral, future work) works because you've just delivered value — this is the peak moment of goodwill
- **Always provide all files and access proactively** — don't make them ask
- **Keep the door open** — many freelancers get repeat business from clean project endings
