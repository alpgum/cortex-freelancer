# Cold Outreach — Email

## Subject Line Options
- "Quick question about [THEIR_COMPANY]'s [SPECIFIC_AREA]"
- "[SPECIFIC_RESULT] for [SIMILAR_COMPANY] — thought of you"
- "Idea for [THEIR_COMPANY]'s [SPECIFIC_CHALLENGE]"

## Email Body

Hi [CLIENT_NAME],

I was looking at [THEIR_COMPANY]'s [WEBSITE/APP/PRODUCT] and noticed [SPECIFIC_OBSERVATION — be genuine and specific, e.g., "your checkout flow has 5 steps where most high-converting sites use 2-3" or "your blog posts rank well but your landing pages aren't showing up for commercial keywords"].

I specialize in [YOUR_SERVICE] for [THEIR_INDUSTRY] companies. Recently, I helped [SIMILAR_COMPANY] [SPECIFIC_RESULT — e.g., "reduce their checkout abandonment rate from 73% to 41%" or "rank on page 1 for 12 commercial keywords in 90 days"].

Would it make sense to chat for 15 minutes about whether I could get similar results for [THEIR_COMPANY]?

If the timing isn't right, no worries at all — happy to share the approach I used for [SIMILAR_COMPANY] either way.

Best,
[YOUR_NAME]
[YOUR_TITLE/SERVICE]
[PORTFOLIO_LINK]

## Follow-Up Sequence

### Follow-Up #1 (Day 3)
Subject: "Re: [ORIGINAL_SUBJECT]"

Hi [CLIENT_NAME], just floating this back up in case it got buried. The [SPECIFIC_OBSERVATION] I mentioned is likely costing [THEIR_COMPANY] [ESTIMATED_IMPACT — e.g., "$X in lost conversions monthly"]. Happy to share more details if useful.

### Follow-Up #2 (Day 7)
Subject: "[RELEVANT_RESOURCE] — thought you'd find this useful"

Hi [CLIENT_NAME], I put together a quick [GUIDE/CHECKLIST/ANALYSIS] on [TOPIC_RELEVANT_TO_THEIR_BUSINESS]. Thought you might find it useful regardless of whether we work together: [LINK]

### Follow-Up #3 (Day 14 — Final)
Subject: "Should I close the loop?"

Hi [CLIENT_NAME], I've reached out a couple of times about [TOPIC]. I don't want to be a pest — if this isn't relevant right now, just say the word and I'll take you off my list. But if the timing is better in Q[NEXT_QUARTER], I'm happy to reconnect then.

## Key Principles
- **Research first:** Spend 10 minutes on their website/LinkedIn before writing
- **Be specific:** Generic outreach gets deleted. Specific observations get responses
- **One CTA:** Don't ask them to reply AND visit your site AND book a call
- **Short:** Under 150 words for the initial email
- **Deliverability:** Use your real name, avoid spam trigger words, don't include more than 1 link
