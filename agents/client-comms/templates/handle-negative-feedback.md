# Handle Negative Feedback

Use when a client expresses dissatisfaction with your work.

---

## Version 1: Specific Criticism (They Told You What's Wrong)

Hi [CLIENT_NAME],

Thank you for the detailed feedback — I appreciate you being specific about what isn't working. That helps me fix it fast.

Let me make sure I understand correctly:

1. [ISSUE_1 — restate their concern in your words]
2. [ISSUE_2]
3. [ISSUE_3]

Here's my plan to address each of these:

1. [SOLUTION_1 — specific action + timeline]
2. [SOLUTION_2]
3. [SOLUTION_3]

I'll have the revised version to you by [DATE]. Does that work?

I want to make sure you're completely happy with the final result. If there's anything else I've missed, please don't hesitate to let me know.

[YOUR_NAME]

---

## Version 2: Vague Dissatisfaction ("It's Not What I Wanted")

Hi [CLIENT_NAME],

I hear you, and I want to get this right. To make sure the next revision hits the mark, could you help me understand specifically:

- What aspects are working well? (So I keep those)
- What feels off? (As specific as possible — colors, layout, tone, functionality?)
- Are there examples of what you had in mind? (A reference site, screenshot, or description would be incredibly helpful)

I want to deliver exactly what you're envisioning. A 15-minute call might be the fastest way to align — would [SUGGESTED_TIME] work?

[YOUR_NAME]

---

## Version 3: Harsh / Emotional Feedback

**Wait at least 30 minutes before responding.** Read their message twice. Separate the emotion from the actionable feedback.

Hi [CLIENT_NAME],

I understand your frustration, and I take this seriously. You're right that the deliverable didn't meet expectations, and I take responsibility for that.

Here's what I'd like to do:

1. **Immediate fix:** I'll address [TOP_PRIORITY_ISSUE] by [DATE — within 24-48 hours]
2. **Full revision:** I'll rework [BROADER_ISSUES] based on your feedback by [DATE]
3. **Alignment call:** I'd like to schedule a brief call to make sure we're 100% on the same page before the next delivery

My goal is to make this right. You hired me because you trusted my work, and I intend to earn that trust back.

Would [SUGGESTED_TIME] work for a quick call?

[YOUR_NAME]

---

## The HEAR Framework
1. **H — Hear them out.** Let them vent. Don't interrupt.
2. **E — Empathize.** "I understand why you feel that way."
3. **A — Acknowledge your part.** Own what you can. "You're right, I should have..."
4. **R — Resolve with options.** Give them a clear fix plan.

## Never Do This
- Respond defensively within minutes of receiving harsh feedback
- Blame the client ("You didn't give clear requirements")
- Make excuses ("I was really busy this week")
- Get into a written argument — suggest a call instead
- Involve platform dispute resolution before trying to fix it directly
