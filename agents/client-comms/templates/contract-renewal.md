# Contract Renewal

Use when a retainer or ongoing contract is approaching its end date.

---

## Version 1: Standard Renewal (Happy Client)

Subject: Renewing our partnership — [MONTH/QUARTER] ahead

Hi [CLIENT_NAME],

Our current [CONTRACT_TYPE — retainer/agreement] wraps up on [END_DATE], and I wanted to get ahead of the renewal conversation.

**What we accomplished this period:**
- [ACHIEVEMENT_1 — e.g., "Launched 3 new landing pages, averaging 4.2% conversion rate"]
- [ACHIEVEMENT_2 — e.g., "Reduced site load time by 40%"]
- [ACHIEVEMENT_3 — e.g., "Resolved 27 support tickets with avg 4-hour turnaround"]

**For the next period, I'd recommend:**
- [PROPOSED_FOCUS_1]
- [PROPOSED_FOCUS_2]
- [PROPOSED_FOCUS_3]

I'd love to continue working together. Here are the renewal options:

1. **Same terms:** [HOURS]/month at [CURRENT_RATE]/month
2. **Expanded scope:** [MORE_HOURS]/month at [HIGHER_RATE]/month — adds [ADDITIONAL_SERVICES]
3. **Reduced scope:** [FEWER_HOURS]/month at [LOWER_RATE]/month — focuses on [CORE_SERVICES]

Want to hop on a quick call to discuss? I'm available [DATE_OPTIONS].

[YOUR_NAME]

## Version 2: Renewal with Rate Increase

Subject: [NEXT_PERIOD] partnership + updated terms

Hi [CLIENT_NAME],

As we approach the end of our current agreement on [END_DATE], I wanted to discuss renewal.

First, here's what we've accomplished:
- [KEY_RESULTS — 2-3 bullets]

I'm excited to continue our work. I do want to be transparent that I'm adjusting my rates for [NEXT_PERIOD]:

**Updated rate:** [NEW_RATE] (up from [OLD_RATE])

This reflects [REASON — e.g., "increased expertise in your tech stack," "expanded scope of services," or "market rate adjustment"]. For context, my rate for new clients is [HIGHER_RATE] — your rate reflects our established relationship.

I'm committed to delivering even more value in the next period. Here's what I'm planning:
- [PROPOSED_VALUE_ADD_1]
- [PROPOSED_VALUE_ADD_2]

Shall we discuss the details? I want to make sure the arrangement works well for both of us.

[YOUR_NAME]

## Version 3: Renewal After a Rough Patch

Subject: Looking ahead — [PROJECT/RELATIONSHIP] renewal

Hi [CLIENT_NAME],

I know the last [TIME_PERIOD] had some bumps, and I appreciate your patience as we worked through [SPECIFIC_ISSUE]. Since then, we've [HOW_YOU_FIXED_IT — e.g., "established clearer communication protocols" or "hit every deadline in the last 6 weeks"].

As our agreement comes up for renewal on [END_DATE], I'd like to propose we continue with [ADJUSTED_TERMS — e.g., "more frequent check-ins" or "a smaller initial commitment with option to expand"]:

- [PROPOSED_TERM_1]
- [PROPOSED_TERM_2]

I value our working relationship and I'm committed to making the next period our strongest yet.

Would a call this week make sense to discuss?

[YOUR_NAME]

---

## Tips
- **Start the conversation 2-3 weeks before expiry** — don't wait until the last day
- **Lead with results** — remind them of the value before discussing money
- **Offer options** — people prefer choosing to being told
- **Rate increases:** Tie them to increased value, not just inflation
