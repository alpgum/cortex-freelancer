## Timing Guide
- **Best time to upsell:** Within 48 hours of a successful delivery (the "glow window")
- **Also good:** During quarterly reviews, at contract renewal, or when they mention a related problem
- **Never upsell:** During disputes, when they're unhappy, or mid-crisis
