# Check-In — Quiet Client

Use when a client has gone silent mid-project (no response for 3-7 days).

---

## Version 1: Gentle (Day 3-4)

Subject: Quick check-in on [PROJECT_NAME]

Hi [CLIENT_NAME],

Hope your week is going well! I wanted to check in on [PROJECT_NAME] — I sent over [WHAT_YOU_SENT — e.g., "the mockup designs" or "the first draft"] on [DATE] and wanted to make sure they landed okay.

I'm ready to move to the next phase once I have your feedback. No rush — just want to keep things on track for our [DEADLINE] target.

Let me know if you have any questions!

[YOUR_NAME]

---

## Version 2: Structured (Day 5-6)

Subject: [PROJECT_NAME] — need your input to stay on schedule

Hi [CLIENT_NAME],

I want to make sure we stay on track for [DEADLINE]. I'm currently waiting on your feedback on:

- [SPECIFIC_ITEM_1 — e.g., "Homepage mockup (sent [DATE])"]
- [SPECIFIC_ITEM_2 — e.g., "Content for the About page"]

To hit our timeline, I'd ideally need your input by [SUGGESTED_DATE]. If your priorities have shifted or you need more time, totally fine — just let me know so I can adjust the schedule accordingly.

Happy to hop on a quick call if that's easier than writing feedback.

Best,
[YOUR_NAME]

---

## Version 3: Direct (Day 7+)

Subject: [PROJECT_NAME] — checking in

Hi [CLIENT_NAME],

I haven't heard back since [DATE_OF_LAST_CONTACT] and want to make sure everything is okay on your end.

I've paused work on [PROJECT_NAME] pending your review of [SPECIFIC_ITEMS]. I'm happy to jump right back in as soon as I hear from you.

If your priorities have changed or you'd like to discuss the project direction, I completely understand — just let me know how you'd like to proceed.

Looking forward to hearing from you.

[YOUR_NAME]

---

## Tips
- **Day 3-4:** Keep it light. They're probably just busy.
- **Day 5-6:** Add structure and a specific date. Makes it easy to respond.
- **Day 7+:** Be direct but not accusatory. Give them an easy out ("priorities changed").
- **Never say:** "Just following up" or "Circling back" — be specific about what you need.
- **Pro tip:** If they go silent on email, try a different channel (Upwork message, text, Slack).
