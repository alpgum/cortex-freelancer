# Introduction After Hire

## Send Within: 1-2 hours of being hired

Subject: Excited to get started — quick intro + next steps

Hi [CLIENT_NAME],

Thank you for choosing to work together — I'm genuinely excited about [PROJECT_NAME/DESCRIPTION].

A quick intro: I'm [YOUR_NAME], and I specialize in [YOUR_SPECIALTY]. I've worked on similar projects for [RELEVANT_EXPERIENCE — e.g., "SaaS companies" or "e-commerce brands"], so I'm confident we'll get great results here.

**Here's how I work:**

1. **Communication:** I'll send you [FREQUENCY — e.g., "weekly"] updates every [DAY]. You can reach me via [PREFERRED_CHANNEL — e.g., "Upwork messages or email"]. I typically respond within [RESPONSE_TIME — e.g., "a few hours during business hours"].

2. **Timeline:** Based on our discussion, here's what I'm planning:
   - [MILESTONE_1] — by [DATE_1]
   - [MILESTONE_2] — by [DATE_2]
   - [FINAL_DELIVERY] — by [FINAL_DATE]

3. **What I need from you to get started:**
   - [ASSET_1 — e.g., "Brand guidelines / logo files"]
   - [ASSET_2 — e.g., "Access to your CMS/staging environment"]
   - [ASSET_3 — e.g., "Any reference sites or examples you like"]

4. **Feedback process:** I'll share work at each milestone for your review. I include [NUMBER] rounds of revisions at each stage.

Would [SUGGESTED_DATE_TIME] work for a quick 15-minute kickoff call? I have a few questions that'll help me deliver exactly what you're looking for.

Looking forward to it!

Best,
[YOUR_NAME]

## Tips
- Send this ASAP after hire — first impressions matter enormously
- Be specific about your process — it builds confidence immediately
- Asking for assets early prevents delays later
- The kickoff call request shows professionalism and reduces misunderstandings
- Keep it warm but structured — you're setting the tone for the entire project
