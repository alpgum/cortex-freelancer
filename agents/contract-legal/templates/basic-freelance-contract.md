## 4. Revisions

## 5. Intellectual Property
All intellectual property created under this agreement shall be assigned to the Client **upon receipt of final payment in full.** Until full payment is received, all IP remains the property of the Freelancer.

**Exclusions:** Pre-existing tools, libraries, frameworks, and methodologies owned by the Freelancer prior to this agreement remain the Freelancer's property. The Client receives a perpetual, non-exclusive license to use these components as part of the delivered work.

**Portfolio rights:** The Freelancer retains the right to display the work in their portfolio, case studies, and marketing materials unless otherwise agreed in writing.

## 6. Confidentiality
Both parties agree to keep confidential information private. Confidential information includes business plans, technical specifications, customer data, and financial information shared during this engagement.

This obligation survives for [2] years after the end of this agreement.

## 7. Termination
Either party may terminate this agreement with [14] days written notice.

**Upon termination:**
- Client pays for all work completed up to the termination date
- If Client terminates without cause, a kill fee of [25]% of the remaining contract value applies
- Freelancer delivers all completed work upon receipt of payment
- If Freelancer terminates, they provide reasonable transition support

## 8. Liability Limitation
The Freelancer's total liability under this agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid under this agreement. The Freelancer is not liable for indirect, consequential, or incidental damages.

## 9. Change Orders
Any work outside the defined scope requires a written Change Order signed by both parties. Change Orders will include a description of the additional work, timeline impact, and cost.

## 10. Dispute Resolution
Both parties agree to attempt to resolve any disputes through good-faith negotiation. If unresolved after [30] days, disputes will be resolved through [ARBITRATION/MEDIATION/COURT] in [JURISDICTION].

This document constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior discussions.
