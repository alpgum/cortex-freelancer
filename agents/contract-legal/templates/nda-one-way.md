# One-Way Non-Disclosure Agreement

> **DISCLAIMER:** This is a template. Consult a qualified attorney for your specific situation.

---

**BETWEEN:**

**Disclosing Party:** [CLIENT_NAME] ("Client")
**Receiving Party:** [YOUR_NAME] ("Freelancer")

**Effective Date:** [DATE]

---

## 1. Purpose
The Client wishes to disclose certain confidential information to the Freelancer for the purpose of [PROJECT_DESCRIPTION].

## 2. Confidential Information
"Confidential Information" means information designated as confidential by the Client or that would reasonably be understood to be confidential, including:
- [SPECIFIC_TYPES — e.g., "Product roadmap, user data, proprietary algorithms"]

**Exclusions:** Information that is publicly available, independently developed by the Freelancer, or rightfully known prior to disclosure.

## 3. Freelancer's Obligations
The Freelancer agrees to:
- Maintain strict confidentiality
- Use the information only for the stated Purpose
- Not disclose to any third party without written consent
- Return or destroy all materials upon project completion or request

## 4. Duration
Confidentiality obligations survive for [2] years after the date of disclosure.

## 5. Permitted Disclosures
The Freelancer may disclose Confidential Information if required by law, provided they give the Client reasonable prior notice.

## 6. Portfolio Rights
Notwithstanding the above, the Freelancer retains the right to [STATE PORTFOLIO RIGHTS — e.g., "mention the Client's name and general project description in their portfolio, without revealing Confidential Information" OR "the Freelancer may not reference this engagement publicly"].

## 7. Governing Law
This agreement is governed by the laws of [JURISDICTION].
