# Follow-Up Message Templates

## Variables
- [CLIENT_NAME] — Client's name
- [PROJECT_DETAIL] — What you proposed on
- [DAYS_AGO] — How long since your proposal
- [NEW_INSIGHT] — Something new you can offer
- [YOUR_NAME] — Your name

---

## Short Variant — Gentle Check-In (3-5 days after proposal)

[CLIENT_NAME],

Just checking in on the [PROJECT_DETAIL] project — I'm still available and interested if you haven't made a decision yet. Happy to answer any questions or adjust my approach based on your feedback.

[YOUR_NAME]

---

## Detailed Variant — Value-Add Follow-Up (5-7 days after proposal)

[CLIENT_NAME],

I wanted to follow up on my proposal for [PROJECT_DETAIL] from [DAYS_AGO] days ago. I understand you're probably evaluating multiple candidates — to help with your decision, I wanted to share something:

[NEW_INSIGHT — choose one of these approaches:]

**Option A — Free insight:**
Since submitting my proposal, I gave your project more thought and noticed [specific observation about their project/business]. Here's what I'd recommend: [brief, actionable suggestion]. This is yours to use regardless of who you hire.

**Option B — Updated approach:**
I've refined my approach since my initial proposal. Specifically, I realized [adjustment] would give you better results because [reason]. Happy to walk you through the updated plan.

**Option C — Social proof:**
I just completed a similar project this week — [brief description]. The client was happy with [result]. I can share the details if it helps you evaluate my fit for your project.

No pressure either way. If you've already hired someone, no worries — I hope the project goes well. If you're still deciding, I'm here and ready to start.

[YOUR_NAME]

---

## Last Follow-Up (10-14 days, final attempt)

[CLIENT_NAME],

Last note from me on the [PROJECT_DETAIL] project. If the timing isn't right or you went with someone else, completely understood. If the project is still open and you'd like to discuss, I'm available.

Either way, best of luck with it.

[YOUR_NAME]
