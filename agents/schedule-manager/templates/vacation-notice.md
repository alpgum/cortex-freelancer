# Vacation / Out-of-Office Notice

## Pre-Vacation Client Message (Send 1-2 Weeks Before)

Subject: Heads up — I'll be OOO [DATE_RANGE]

Hi [CLIENT_NAME],

I wanted to give you advance notice that I'll be out of office from **[START_DATE]** to **[END_DATE]**.

**Before I leave, I'll make sure:**
- [DELIVERABLE_1 — e.g., "The homepage design is finalized and handed off"]
- [DELIVERABLE_2 — e.g., "All pending feedback is addressed"]
- [MILESTONE — e.g., "We're in a clean stopping point with no loose ends"]

**During my absence:**
- I will not be checking messages or email
- For genuine emergencies: [EMERGENCY_CONTACT — e.g., "text me at +[NUMBER]" or "contact [BACKUP_PERSON]"]
- I'll be back and fully online on **[RETURN_DATE]**

**When I return:**
- I'll review anything you've sent and respond by [RETURN_DATE + 1 day]
- We can pick up exactly where we left off

Is there anything you need from me before [START_DATE] to keep things moving on your end?

[YOUR_NAME]

---

## Auto-Reply / OOO Message

Subject: Out of Office — Back [RETURN_DATE]

Hi there,

Thanks for your message. I'm currently out of office from [START_DATE] to [END_DATE] with limited access to email.

I'll respond to your message when I return on [RETURN_DATE].

For urgent matters, please contact [BACKUP_CONTACT].

Best,
[YOUR_NAME]

---

## Tips
- Give at least 2 weeks' notice for vacations longer than 3 days
- Front-load work before leaving — deliver early if possible
- Set clear expectations: you will NOT be checking messages
- Have a clean handoff point — don't leave clients mid-deliverable
- Block your calendar immediately when you decide on dates
- You deserve time off. Don't apologize for taking it.
