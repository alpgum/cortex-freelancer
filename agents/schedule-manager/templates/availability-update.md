# Availability Update

## Reduced Availability

Subject: Updated availability — [DATE_RANGE]

Hi [CLIENT_NAME],

Quick heads up — my availability is changing for [DATE_RANGE]:

**Updated hours:** [NEW_HOURS — e.g., "Monday-Thursday, 10 AM - 3 PM EET"]
**Reason:** [BRIEF_REASON — e.g., "Taking a training course" or "Family commitment"]
**Impact on your project:** [HONEST_ASSESSMENT — e.g., "No impact — I've front-loaded the work" or "The mockup delivery may shift from Friday to Monday"]

**What stays the same:**
- I'll still respond to messages within [RESPONSE_TIME]
- Emergency issues: reach me at [EMERGENCY_CONTACT]
- All current deadlines will be met

Let me know if you have any concerns or if we need to adjust anything.

[YOUR_NAME]

## Increased Availability (Taking on More Work)

Subject: Opening up availability — [MONTH/QUARTER]

Hi [CLIENT_NAME],

Good news — I have some additional bandwidth opening up in [TIMEFRAME]. If you have any upcoming projects or extra work you've been thinking about, I'd love to discuss.

My current availability: [HOURS_PER_WEEK] hours/week for new work, starting [DATE].

Anything on your radar?

[YOUR_NAME]

---

## Tips
- Give at least 1 week's notice for availability changes
- Always address the impact on their project specifically
- Provide an emergency contact method
- Never over-explain personal reasons — a brief mention is sufficient
