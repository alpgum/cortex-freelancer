# Meeting Request

## Standard Meeting Request

Subject: Quick call — [TOPIC]?

Hi [CLIENT_NAME],

I'd like to schedule a brief [DURATION — 15/25 minutes] call to discuss [TOPIC — e.g., "the project kickoff" or "feedback on the mockups"].

Here are a few time options in your timezone ([CLIENT_TIMEZONE]):

- [OPTION_1 — e.g., "Tuesday, March 25 at 10:00 AM EST"]
- [OPTION_2 — e.g., "Wednesday, March 26 at 2:00 PM EST"]
- [OPTION_3 — e.g., "Thursday, March 27 at 11:00 AM EST"]

If none of these work, feel free to pick a time that suits you: [BOOKING_LINK — e.g., Cal.com or Calendly link]

**Agenda:**
1. [AGENDA_ITEM_1]
2. [AGENDA_ITEM_2]
3. Next steps

Talk soon!
[YOUR_NAME]

---

## Tips
- Always present times in the CLIENT'S timezone first
- Offer 3 options across different days/times
- Keep to 15-25 minutes — never default to 60
- Always include an agenda — no-agenda meetings waste everyone's time
- Include a booking link as a backup option
- Send the invite at least 48 hours in advance
