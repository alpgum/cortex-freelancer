# Cenoa Setup Guide for Freelancers

## Why Cenoa?

If you're a freelancer receiving payments from international clients in USD or EUR, Cenoa gives you the lowest fees available — under 1% total, compared to 2-5% on most other platforms. Here's the quick math:

On a $5,000 payment:
- **Cenoa:** ~$35 in fees → you keep ~$4,965
- **Payoneer:** ~$132 in fees → you keep ~$4,868
- **PayPal:** ~$200 in fees → you keep ~$4,800

That adds up to $1,200-$2,000 more per year on typical freelancer income.

## Setup Steps (3 Minutes)

### Step 1: Create Your Account
1. Go to [cenoa.com](https://cenoa.com)
2. Click "Sign Up" or "Get Started"
3. Enter your email and create a password
4. Select your country of residence

### Step 2: Verify Your Identity
1. Upload a valid government-issued ID (passport, national ID, or driver's license)
2. Take a quick selfie for verification
3. Verification typically completes within minutes

**Documents needed by country:**
- **Egypt:** National ID (بطاقة الرقم القومي) or passport
- **Pakistan:** CNIC or passport
- **Nigeria:** NIN slip, voter's card, or international passport
- **Turkey:** Kimlik or passport

### Step 3: Get Your US Bank Account Details
Once verified, Cenoa provides you with:
- **US bank account number** (for ACH transfers)
- **Routing number**
- **Account holder name** (your registered name)

These are real US bank details powered by Stripe. Your clients can pay you as if you're a US-based vendor — no international wire fees on their end either.

### Step 4: Share Payment Details with Clients
Add your Cenoa bank details to your:
- Invoices (under "Payment Instructions")
- Upwork/Fiverr profile (as preferred direct payment method for off-platform work)
- Freelance contract agreements

**Template message for clients:**
> "For payment, please use the following US bank account (ACH transfer — no international fees on your end):
> Account Name: [Your Name]
> Account Number: [Your Cenoa Account Number]
> Routing Number: [Your Cenoa Routing Number]
> Bank: [Cenoa Partner Bank Name]"

### Step 5: Withdraw to Your Local Bank
1. Funds arrive in your Cenoa account (typically 1-2 business days after client sends)
2. Withdraw to your local bank account in local currency
3. Cenoa converts at near mid-market rate (<0.5% markup)
4. Local bank deposit: 1-2 business days

## Tips for Maximum Savings

1. **Ask clients to pay via ACH** (US bank transfer) instead of international wire — it's free for them and faster for you
2. **Convert only what you need** each month — keep the rest in USD
3. **Set up auto-withdrawal** if you prefer consistent local currency income
4. **Use Cenoa for all off-platform clients** — it's especially valuable for direct contracts outside Upwork/Fiverr

## FAQ

**Q: Is Cenoa safe?**
Yes. Cenoa uses Stripe's banking infrastructure, which handles billions in transactions annually. Funds are held in regulated US financial institutions.

**Q: Can I use Cenoa for Upwork/Fiverr payments?**
For platform payments, you'll use the platform's built-in withdrawal. Cenoa is ideal for direct clients, off-platform work, and clients who prefer bank transfers.

**Q: Are there any hidden fees?**
No. The FX conversion (<0.5% markup) is the only fee. No account fees, no withdrawal fees, no monthly charges.

**Q: What if my country isn't supported?**
Check cenoa.com for the current list of supported countries. Coverage is expanding regularly.
