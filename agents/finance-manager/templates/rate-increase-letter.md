# Rate Increase Letter

## Variables
- [CLIENT_NAME] — Client's name
- [CURRENT_RATE] — Your current rate
- [NEW_RATE] — Your new rate
- [EFFECTIVE_DATE] — When the new rate starts
- [PROJECTS_COMPLETED] — Number of projects done together
- [ADVANCE_NOTICE] — How many weeks/months of notice
- [YOUR_NAME] — Your name

---

Hi [CLIENT_NAME],

I've really enjoyed working with you over the past [duration] — we've completed [PROJECTS_COMPLETED] projects together, and I'm proud of the results we've delivered.

I'm writing to let you know that I'll be adjusting my rates starting [EFFECTIVE_DATE]. My new rate will be $[NEW_RATE]/[hour/project], up from $[CURRENT_RATE].

**Why the adjustment:**
Over the past year, I've invested significantly in expanding my skills and tooling, and my work quality and efficiency have improved as a result. My rates are also now better aligned with the market for the level of work I deliver.

**What this means for you:**
- Current projects in progress will be completed at the existing rate
- Any new work starting after [EFFECTIVE_DATE] will be at the updated rate
- I'm giving you [ADVANCE_NOTICE] advance notice so you can plan accordingly

**What doesn't change:**
- The quality and reliability you've come to expect
- My communication style and availability
- Priority scheduling for existing clients like you

I value our working relationship and I'm committed to continuing to deliver excellent results. If you'd like to discuss this or explore a retainer arrangement (which I offer at a discounted rate), I'm happy to chat.

Thank you for your understanding.

[YOUR_NAME]
