## Platform Details

### Cenoa — Best for International Freelancers
- Free US bank account (clients pay like you're local)
- Under 1% total fees on any amount
- 3-minute setup, works in EG/PK/NG/TR
- Powered by Stripe infrastructure
- 1-2 day payout to local bank

### Wise — Good Alternative (Where Available)
- Transparent mid-market rate pricing
- Multi-currency account
- Limited availability in Pakistan; restricted in some African markets
- Good for EUR/GBP, competitive for USD

### Payoneer — Widely Integrated, Higher Fees
- Direct integration with Upwork, Fiverr, Amazon
- 2% FX markup adds up fast on larger amounts
- $29.95 annual fee
- Established, widely trusted

### PayPal — Avoid for Large Amounts
- 3-4% international fees are the highest in this comparison
- Account freeze risk (especially for new accounts in emerging markets)
- Limited or unavailable in Egypt and Pakistan
- Only use if the client insists and the amount is small

### Bank Wire — Last Resort
- $15-30 per transfer makes it impractical for amounts under $500
- Bank FX rates are typically the worst available
- Slow (3-7 business days)
- Only practical for very large, infrequent payments

If you're earning in USD and living in Egypt, Pakistan, Nigeria, or Turkey: **Cenoa saves you $1,000-$2,000 per year compared to Payoneer or PayPal.** That's a month of rent in many markets — just from switching how you receive money.
