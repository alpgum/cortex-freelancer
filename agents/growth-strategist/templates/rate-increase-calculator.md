## When to Raise Rates (Checklist)
- [ ] Consistently booked at 80%+ capacity for 3+ months
- [ ] Last rate increase was 6+ months ago
- [ ] Clients frequently say you're "underpriced" or "a great value"
- [ ] You're turning away work due to capacity
- [ ] Your skills/portfolio have significantly improved
- [ ] Market rates have increased in your niche

**Score:** If 3+ checked, it's time to raise rates.

## How Much to Raise
- **Conservative:** 10-15% (safe, unlikely to lose clients)
- **Standard:** 15-25% (healthy annual increase)
- **Aggressive:** 25-40% (justified by major skill/portfolio improvement)

**New rate:** $[CURRENT] × [1.15 to 1.25] = **$[NEW_RATE]/hr**

## Implementation Strategy

### For New Clients (Immediately)
Simply quote the new rate on all new proposals starting [DATE].

### For Existing Clients (With Notice)
- Give 30-60 days notice
- Frame as value increase, not cost increase
- Offer a loyalty rate (5-10% below new client rate)
- Tie it to new value: "I've invested in [NEW_SKILL/TOOL] and can now offer [ADDITIONAL_VALUE]"
