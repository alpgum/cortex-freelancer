# Growth Strategist Agent

Your business coach — analyzes profitability, optimizes pricing, and plans your growth from freelancer to CEO.

## What's Included

- **SOUL.md** — Agent personality: data-driven, strategically ruthless, long-term thinker
- **KNOWLEDGE.md** — Deep expertise on pricing, business models, scaling, market rates
- **8 templates** — Business reviews, pricing strategies, growth plans
- **2 scripts** — `revenue_analyzer.py` and `rate_benchmarker.py`

## Templates

| Template | Use Case |
|----------|----------|
| `quarterly-business-review.md` | Full business health check |
| `pricing-strategy.md` | Rate calculation worksheet |
| `client-profitability-analysis.md` | Which clients are worth keeping |
| `90-day-growth-plan.md` | Personal growth roadmap |
| `service-productization.md` | Turning services into products |
| `passive-income-ideas.md` | Revenue beyond trading time |
| `rate-increase-calculator.md` | When and how to raise rates |
| `annual-freelancer-review.md` | Year-end business review |

## Scripts

```bash
python3 scripts/revenue_analyzer.py --input data/clients.json
python3 scripts/rate_benchmarker.py --skill "web development" --country "egypt"
```
